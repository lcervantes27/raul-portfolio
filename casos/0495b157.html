Context

What is Yalo?

Yalo's mission

Yalo, partnered with Meta, provides automation and user behavioral data for LATAM industries to improve their CRM and the “WhatsApp for Business” product. The systems made for the "Consumer Packed Goods" and "Retail" categories often require e-commerce platforms for them to be adopted by their staff and customers.

Given the Yalo’s history and its growth as a startup, it was important for the Retail product to consider each of the slight variants that are required by the industry: Selling combos, adding items to an order, customize different dishes, different hierarchical levels to organize 50, 100, 500 or thousands of products, among others.

Project's Objective

The main objective of this project was to automate customer service for Pollo Campero, but by thinking and designing it as a system, we were able to replicate our success for different customers.

My Role

Product & Interaction Design Team Lead

As a Product Designer on the “Support & Improvements” area, I was responsible of providing solutions to our “Conversational UX Designers” for them to build the best possible interaction systems, mostly by taking the ownership of a chatbot project myself and prioritizing its consistency, scalability, analyze its metrics and documenting the results with “Best Practices” insights for the related industry, being retail, in this case.

Process

Design tools and methods

🤖 Bot personality
(Research + Definition)

This process was defined prior to this project. As we constantly needed to negotiate the need of a Design Research process on our first projects, this deliverable was designed to comunicate our stakeholders the value of understanding their clients to connect with them through a familiar interaction.

It sets the bases for the tone and type of responses that can be designed to adapt the conversation to the context through which the user is navigating without losing the consistency of the conversational design. It is usually based on brand manuals, social media strategy and all the info we can gather about the users of the tool.

By analyzing stakeholder expectations, worker processes, and user needs for WhatsApp meal ordering and customization, I identified patterns that were applicable to other design and product teams facing similar challenges, so I proposed a series of internal workshops so we could be able to connect the dots of the whole system, share findings, and advance towards the same goal.

📐 Flowchart + Wireframe + Wireflow

Recognizing the unique strengths of the graphical interface compared to the current conversational interface, I designed the interaction to feel like one unified experience, preventing it from seeming like a workaround.

This document was also needed to negotiate the required time and resources with Yalo's and Pollo Campero's leads for our expected goal.

🛒 GUI design System for e-commerce

This modular web app system provides four base variations that can be tailored for different needs, with optional features for Product Teams to meet the unique requirements of each project.

Using Atomic Design principles, we achieve standardized quality for our web apps, significantly shorten the design cycle, and improve client negotiation outcomes.

This system is often used in e-commerce for grocery shopping, food delivery, restocking stores and more.

First set of mockups so more team members with different perspectives could participate on Co-Creation workshops
