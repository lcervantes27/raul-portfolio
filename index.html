👨‍🚀 Hello there!

(... General Kenobi)

I'm a Senior Product Designer currently based on México City.

I have been designing for the tech industry for around 10 years (!), in which I had the opportunity to collaborate with different emerging technologies, such as AR, VR, Big Data, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Conversational Assistants, a new focus on Internet browsing, e-commerce platforms and gaming related products.

I've been genuinely passionate about new technologies (and all the geeky stuff related to them) since I was a kid; this excitement drives me to stay informed about industry trends and continually explore new products and interaction systems.

That's why, In every project I participate in, I strive not only to meet immediate goals but also to contribute to the larger tech ecosystem, whether it's engaging with veteran power users or welcoming newcomers into this exciting world! :)

Career highlights

Some of the key moments I enjoyed being part of

Contribution on Windows 11’s launch

Recognition from the Windows 11 leadership team after working on a set of concepts for AI-powered productivity features on the OS, which involved the usage of their integration system between the Desktop section, its widgets and M365 products.

Design intervention’s impact

Had the opportunity to work along with impressive multidisciplinary teams on beloved & widely used products which had a big impact on their user niche.

From conversational experiences that helped people to understand how to take care of their health and adopt new behaviour (by ordering their groseries with the help of digital products) during the Covid-19 pandemic confinement, reaching over 470,000 monthly users.

To some others with inmense user bases, such as Microsoft Edge and Windows 11, with productivity AI-features that reached over 46 million people on its first month.

Featured success stories on Facebook for Business’s blog

75% decrease in order processing time and 16% increase in orders placed through a chatbot on Whatsapp for a popular fried chicken fast food chain, called Pollo Campero, with restaurants on 12 different countries.

Automation of 45% of customer service conversations through WhatsApp for a hospital company in South America called Blue Medical. More than 70,000 questions resolved and 9,000 appointments scheduled through the chatbot during this pandemic event. This case was submited by Yalo as a success case story for Facebook Business website.

mentor & leadership experience

I was selected to lead a team of 8 designers after I offered a detailed work plan to introduce Human-Centered-Design for an international software company called Virtualware. We developed 3 projects in a span of 1.5 years, involving web, mobile, AR, VR and Big Data technologies.

Held “Design Talks” and educative material to promote Human-Centered practices, involving topics like: Design Thinking, Business Innovation, Methodologies, Complex-Thinking models, Prospective practices, etc.
